Screw Pile Footing FAQ

Everything you need to know about screw pile footings in Victoria — from costs and installation to soil suitability and council approvals.

Screw piles (also called helical piles or ground screws) are steel shafts with helical plates that are mechanically screwed into the ground to create a solid footing. They replace traditional concrete footings and can be built on immediately after installation — no curing time required. Easy Footings installs three product ranges: RapidStump, StumpRite, and SurePile.
Screw piles install in hours rather than days, require no excavation or concrete trucks, are unaffected by weather, produce zero waste, and can be built on immediately. Concrete footings require excavation, formwork, pouring, and 7–28 days of curing time. For most residential projects in Victoria, screw piles are faster, cleaner, and more cost-effective. Read our full comparison guide.
Screw piles are used for deck footings, shed foundations, granny flat and secondary dwelling foundations, house restumping, new home builds, retaining walls, pergolas, carports, and commercial structures. They suit almost any project that requires a footing — from a backyard deck to a large commercial building.
Most residential projects are completed in a single day. A typical deck might take 2–4 hours, a shed 1–3 hours, and a full house restump 1–2 days. You can begin building immediately after the piles are installed — there's no waiting for concrete to cure.
A building permit is required for any structural work — the footing system is part of that permit. Some smaller projects like low decks and garden sheds may be exempt, but exemptions vary by council. Always check with your local council or building surveyor before starting work. See our council approval guide for more detail.
Screw piles work in most Victorian soil types including clay, sand, loam, and fill. They are particularly effective in Melbourne's reactive clay soils because they anchor below the reactive zone into stable ground, avoiding the heave and settlement problems that affect shallow concrete footings. Read more about screw piles for reactive clay.
Screw pile costs typically range from $80 to $200 per pile fully installed, depending on pile diameter, depth, soil conditions, and site access. A deck project might cost $500–$2,000 for footings, while a full house restump could range from $2,400–$7,000. Contact Easy Footings for a free site assessment and accurate quote.
