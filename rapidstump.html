What is RapidStump?

RapidStump is an engineered screw pile footing system designed and manufactured by BMSA Footing Solutions. It replaces traditional concrete footings with a steel screw pile that is mechanically driven into the ground, providing immediate load-bearing capacity the moment installation is complete.

Unlike concrete stumps that require excavation, formwork, pouring, and 7–28 days of curing time, RapidStump footings are installed in minutes per pile. This makes them the preferred choice for Victorian builders who need to keep projects on schedule regardless of Melbourne's unpredictable weather.

The system is fully engineer-certified and meets all Australian building standards, giving you confidence that your foundations are as strong — and often stronger — than traditional concrete alternatives. Easy Footings supplies and installs RapidStump across Melbourne, Geelong, Ballarat, Bendigo, and all of Victoria.

No curing time. Start framing, decking, or construction the moment the last pile goes in. Zero downtime between footings and building.
