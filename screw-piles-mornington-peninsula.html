Why Peninsula Builders Choose Screw Piles

The Mornington Peninsula is one of Victoria's most active building regions, with a unique mix of holiday home construction, deck builds, renovations, and new residential development. The Peninsula's sandy and coastal soils, combined with sloping terrain and environmentally sensitive coastal zones, make it ideally suited to screw pile foundations.

Deck footings are the number one application on the Peninsula. Whether you're building a new entertainer's deck at a Mornington weekender, replacing a weathered deck in Sorrento, or adding outdoor living space to a Dromana holiday home, RapidStump screw pile footings provide fast, clean, permanent foundations without concrete trucks on your property.

For sloping coastal blocks — common from Mount Martha through to Portsea — screw piles are the ideal foundation. Their adjustable height handles any slope without the expense of cut-and-fill earthworks or retaining walls. And with zero concrete, there's no risk of runoff into sensitive coastal waterways.
