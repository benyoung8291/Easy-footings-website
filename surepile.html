What is SurePile?

SurePile is the heavy-duty helical pile system from BMSA Footing Solutions, engineered for projects that demand superior load-bearing capacity. Where RapidStump excels for residential applications like decks and sheds, SurePile handles the big jobs — commercial buildings, large homes, industrial structures, and challenging ground conditions.

Helical piles work by screwing a steel shaft with welded helical plates deep into the ground, where they anchor into stable soil below the active zone. This makes SurePile particularly effective in Victoria's reactive clay soils, where traditional concrete footings are prone to heaving and settlement.

Easy Footings supplies and installs SurePile across Melbourne, Geelong, Ballarat, Bendigo, and throughout regional Victoria.

Why Choose SurePile?

Heavy-duty performance meets the speed and sustainability of screw pile technology.

Superior Load Capacity

Engineered helical plates provide exceptional bearing capacity for heavy commercial and structural loads, verified by torque monitoring during installation.

Rapid Installation

Even large-scale projects install significantly faster than bored piers or concrete footings, reducing project timelines and site costs.

All-Weather Capable

No concrete curing means no weather delays. Your commercial project stays on schedule regardless of Victoria's changeable conditions.

Deep Foundation Reach

SurePile can be extended to reach stable bearing strata at significant depth, making it ideal for challenging soil conditions including reactive clay.

Low Environmental Impact

Zero concrete, minimal excavation, and low vibration installation. Perfect for sites near existing structures or in environmentally sensitive areas.

Torque-Verified

Every pile is torque-monitored during installation, providing verified load capacity data for engineering sign-off and council compliance.
