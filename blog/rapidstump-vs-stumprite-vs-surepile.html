RapidStump vs StumpRite vs SurePile: Which Footing System Is Right for Your Project?

Compare the three leading BMSA concrete-free footing systems to find the best match for your soil, loads, and project type.

Published 1 January 2026 · Updated 1 April 2026

BMSA Footing Solutions manufactures three distinct concrete-free footing systems, each engineered for specific applications and conditions. As Victoria's specialist BMSA installer, Easy Footings helps builders and homeowners choose the right system for every project.

In this guide, we compare all three products — RapidStump, StumpRite, and SurePile — across key factors including load capacity, installation method, best applications, and soil suitability.

Overview of BMSA Footing Systems

All three BMSA systems share common advantages over traditional concrete footings: zero curing time, all-weather installation, adjustable height, and full engineer certification. Where they differ is in their installation method, load capacity, and ideal applications. For a broader comparison with concrete, see our concrete-free vs concrete guide.

RapidStump: Fast & Versatile

RapidStump is BMSA's most versatile footing system, designed for fast installation across a wide range of residential applications.

  • Installation method — mechanically driven/installed using hydraulic equipment
  • Speed — the fastest system to install, ideal for high-volume residential work
  • Adjustability — height-adjustable post-installation for precision levelling
  • Best applications — decks, pergolas, home extensions, granny flats, sheds, light structures
  • Ideal soil types — performs well in Class A through H1 soils
  • Strengths — speed of installation, versatility, cost-effectiveness for standard loads

RapidStump is the go-to choice for most residential deck and light structural projects across Melbourne and Victoria.

StumpRite: Precision Restumping

StumpRite is specifically engineered for house restumping (reblocking) applications.

  • Installation method — designed for confined subfloor spaces common in older Melbourne homes
  • Precision — fine height adjustment system for precise re-levelling of existing structures
  • Adjustability — the most precise height adjustment of the three systems
  • Best applications — restumping weatherboard homes, Victorian-era houses, post-war brick homes, any raised-floor dwelling
  • Ideal soil types — all Victorian soil types, with engineer specification for reactive clays
  • Strengths — precision, tight-access capability, designed specifically for restumping

If you're restumping an older Melbourne home, StumpRite is likely the best choice. Its design addresses the specific challenges of working in low-clearance subfloor spaces while achieving precise house levelling.

SurePile: Heavy-Duty Screw Piles

SurePile is BMSA's heavy-duty screw pile system, engineered for maximum load capacity and depth.

  • Installation method — helical screw pile driven by hydraulic torque head
  • Capacity — the highest load capacity of the three systems
  • Depth — can reach significant depths, ideal for reactive clay and deep bearing requirements
  • Best applications — new builds, heavy structures, commercial buildings, large decks with spas, roofed pergolas, retaining walls
  • Ideal soil types — all types including highly reactive clay (H2, E), sandy soils, and difficult ground conditions
  • Strengths — load capacity, depth capability, performance in challenging soils

SurePile is the premium choice for projects requiring maximum load capacity or deep foundation support — particularly in Melbourne's western and northern suburbs where Class H and E reactive clay is common.

Choosing by Project Type

  • Deck or pergola → RapidStump (or SurePile for heavy loads like spas)
  • House restumping → StumpRite
  • New build → SurePile
  • Home extension → RapidStump (standard) or SurePile (heavy loads)
  • Granny flat → RapidStump or SurePile
  • Shed or outbuilding → RapidStump
  • Commercial structure → SurePile

Choosing by Soil Type

  • Stable/sandy soil (Class A/S) — any system works well; RapidStump for speed
  • Moderate clay (Class M/H1) — RapidStump or StumpRite for standard applications; SurePile for heavy loads
  • Highly reactive clay (Class H2/E) — SurePile is the best choice, with deep helical piles anchoring below the reactive zone
  • Problem soils (Class P) — SurePile with specific engineering design

See our Victorian footing regulations guide for more on soil classifications.

All Systems Are Engineer Certified

Every BMSA product — RapidStump, StumpRite, and SurePile — is fully engineer-certified and compliant with Australian building standards. For each project, a structural engineer reviews the soil report and specifies the appropriate system, size, and installation depth. Frequently Asked Questions

Which BMSA system is best for deck footings?
RapidStump is the most popular choice for standard residential decks. For larger or heavily loaded decks (spas, roofed pergolas), SurePile may be specified for its greater load capacity.
Which system is best for house restumping?
StumpRite is specifically designed for restumping. Its precision height adjustment and tight-access design make it ideal for re-levelling older Melbourne homes.
Can I mix different BMSA systems on one project?
Yes. An engineer may specify different systems for different parts of a project based on varying load requirements. The engineer will specify the right combination for your site.
Are all three systems engineer certified?
Yes. RapidStump, StumpRite, and SurePile are all fully engineer-certified and compliant with Australian building standards.
