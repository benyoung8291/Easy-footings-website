What is StumpRite?

StumpRite is an engineered steel stumping system from BMSA Footing Solutions, designed as a modern replacement for traditional concrete stumps. It is the go-to solution for house restumping across Victoria, eliminating the need for concrete while providing superior adjustability and longevity.

Thousands of older Victorian homes — particularly in Melbourne, Ballarat, and Bendigo — were built on timber or early concrete stumps that deteriorate over time, causing uneven floors, cracking walls, and sticking doors. StumpRite provides a permanent, maintenance-free replacement that is installed without concrete and can be precisely adjusted for perfect levelling.

Easy Footings supplies and installs StumpRite across all of Victoria, from inner-city Melbourne heritage homes to regional properties in Ballarat, Bendigo, and beyond.

Why Choose StumpRite?

The modern alternative to concrete stumps, engineered for Victorian conditions.

Precision Adjustable

Fine-tune floor levels after installation. If your home settles over time, StumpRite stumps can be re-adjusted — something concrete stumps cannot do.

Build Immediately

No concrete curing. Your builder can start framing and floor work the same day the stumps are installed, saving weeks on your project timeline.

Engineer Certified

Fully engineered to Australian building standards. Meets all Victorian council requirements for residential foundation work.

All-Weather Install

Rain or shine, StumpRite goes in. Melbourne's winter weather will never delay your restumping project again.

Eco Friendly

Zero concrete means zero concrete carbon emissions. Less excavation and waste means less environmental impact on your property.

Cost Effective

Reduced labour and eliminated weather delays mean restumping with StumpRite often costs less overall than traditional concrete stumps.
