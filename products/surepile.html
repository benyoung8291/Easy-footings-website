SurePile

Modular Building Tie Down Anchors

The SurePile tie down is a fully engineered, fast and effective method of securing portable buildings, shipping containers and relocatable buildings against uplift forces in cyclonic conditions.

The simple installation method is quick and effective with minimal ground disruption. Installed according to supplied instructions, it is a fully engineered and certified solution providing instantaneous holding capacity.
