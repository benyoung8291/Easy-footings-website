StumpRite

Adjustable Steel House Stumps & Piers

StumpRite are fully certified Australian made adjustable house stumps and piers offering a screw height adjustment of up to 120mm.

The StumpRite range includes adjustable stump tops, top plates, bases, bracing systems and stair stringers, all manufactured locally from high quality hot dip galvanised steel.
