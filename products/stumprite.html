StumpRite Adjustable Steel Stumps & Adjustable Footings

Australian-made adjustable steel stumps, piers & footings

StumpRite is a fully certified range of adjustable steel stumps and adjustable footings, Australian-made and offering up to 120mm of screw height adjustment. The result is a fast, accurate, dead-level house stump or pier on every site.

The StumpRite range covers everything you need for adjustable steel footings - adjustable stump tops, six top-plate styles, bolt-in, concrete-in and screw-in bases, bracing systems and stair stringers. Every component is manufactured locally from high-quality hot-dip galvanised steel and engineered to Australian standards.
